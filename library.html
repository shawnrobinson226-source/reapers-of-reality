Reading Path

Start where the loop is easiest to see.

If you are new to Reapers of Reality, begin with The Way of the Reaper or The Book of Becoming. Keep The Reaper's Field Manual close by once a pattern is active — it is built to be used, not read straight through. Move into TRUTHCIPHER and The Reaper's Black Book when you are ready for the deeper mirrors and the symbolic layer.

No book replaces another. Each one meets you at a different distance from the pattern.
